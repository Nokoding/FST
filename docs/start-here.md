# Start here

## Open it

The app lives at **https://nokoding.github.io/FST/**. Opening that link is
enough to try it. Nothing to download and nothing to sign up for.

## Put it on your phone or computer

You do not have to, but it is better installed. It gets its own icon, opens
without the browser bars in the way, and keeps working when you have no signal.

**iPhone and iPad.** Open the link in Safari, tap the Share button, then scroll
down to **Add to Home Screen**. Safari never offers to do this on its own, so
this is the only route.

**Android.** Chrome usually offers to install it after a few seconds. If it
does not, open the browser menu and pick **Install app**. There is also an
Install button in the app's own Settings tab.

**Windows, Mac and Linux.** In Chrome or Edge, an install icon appears at the
right hand end of the address bar. Click it. Safari on a Mac can do it from the
File menu with **Add to Dock**.

## The first time you open it

It starts empty, because it is your list and not mine. A short walkthrough
shows up on the first run and covers the whole app in about a minute. You can
skip it at any point, and it will not come back afterwards. If you skipped it
and want it later, Settings has a button to run it again.

What you do first:

1. **Make a page.** A page is a group of people. One page is fine to start.
   Plenty of people only ever have one.
2. **Add someone.** They get a panel with their name, a color, and the two
   buttons.
3. **Tap `+`** when you spend real time with them. That is the whole job.

Everything else, resets and charts and profile pictures and sync, can wait
until you feel like it. [Using it](using-it.md) covers the lot.
