# Using it

## Pages

A page is a group of people. The tabs along the top of the screen are your
pages, and the `+` at the end of that strip makes a new one. Type the name
right there and it opens.

Split them however you like. By where you talk to someone, by how close they
are, or not at all. One page works fine.

Settings has the same thing under **Pages**, where you can also rename or
delete one. Deleting a page deletes the people on it, so be sure.

## Panels

Everyone on a page gets a panel, with a torn comic edge between them. Tap a
panel and it grows, squeezing the others. Tap it again to let go.

Panels drop detail as they get narrower, so a page with eight people still
works. The ones you are not looking at shrink to a name and a score.

To fill the screen with one person, use the corner button, or double tap the
panel. That view shows their banner, their tags and whatever you wrote about
them, and it is where the Customize button lives. Escape closes it.

Settings can change how panels behave. **Split panels** is the default, where
everyone is on screen at once. The other three show one person at a time and
differ only in how they move between them.

## Counting

`+` when you spend time with someone. `-` if you tapped it by mistake.

Each person has two numbers. The big one is this period. Under it, once the
panel is wide enough, is the all time total and how long it has been since you
last logged anything.

The minus will not take a period below zero. It also cannot take the all time
total down below what it should be, so an undo is an undo and not a way to go
negative.

## When the counter resets

Under **Counting** in Settings, pick weekly, monthly, every so many days, or
never. Weekly lets you choose the day. Monthly lets you choose the date.

A reset does two things. It puts this period's numbers into **Past periods** on
the dashboard, and it sets everyone back to zero for the new period. All time
totals are never touched by any of this.

There is a **Reset now and file it** button if you want to start a fresh period
today, and a small **zero the period** link next to each person if you only
want to clear one of them.

## The dashboard

Six ways to draw the same numbers: a pie, a donut, a disk map, bars, rings and
a radar. Pick whichever one makes the point. They all read the same data.

Two switches sit under the chart types. **This period** or **All time**, and
**This page** or **Everyone**.

Below the chart:

- Three totals, including the gap between the person you see most and the
  person you see least. That gap turns red at five, which is a nudge and
  nothing more.
- A breakdown bar per person, sorted by the same numbers the chart uses.
- Past periods, most recent first, once you have had a reset.

## Making someone's panel theirs

Open a person full screen and hit **Customize**. Settings has the same button
next to each name.

- **Gradient.** Ten ready made ones, or build your own from two colors and an
  angle.
- **Text color.** It picks ink or paper automatically based on the background.
  Override it if the automatic choice looks wrong.
- **Picture and banner.** The picture sits at the top of the panel. The banner
  fills the background when that person is full screen. Both get shrunk when
  you add them, so a huge photo will not eat your storage.
- **Frame and effect.** A frame goes around the picture. An effect is an
  animation over the whole panel. Effects need animation switched on in
  Settings.
- **Name style and tags.** Four ways to draw the name, and comma separated tags
  that show as little badges under it.
- **About them.** A few lines that show on the full screen view. Timezone, what
  you two do, anything you want to remember.

## Backing up

Settings, **Data**, **Export a backup**. That saves one file with every page,
person, picture and count in it. **Import a backup** on another device puts it
all back.

Do this once in a while even if you have sync on. A backup is a file you hold,
which is a different kind of safe from a copy in the cloud.

**Erase everything** is at the bottom of Settings and does exactly that, with
no undo.

## Two devices

Sync is off until you turn it on. Once it is on, counts made on your phone with
no signal and counts made on your laptop at the same time both survive, because
of how the merge works. [Sync](sync.md) covers the setup, and
[how it works inside](architecture.md) covers why nothing gets lost.
